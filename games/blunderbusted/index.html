About the Game

Blunderbusted is a roguelike gallery shooter game that I'm working on with Dark Tonic. This is a commercial game listed on steam, so you can't play it here.

Below are some screenshots of my in-engine work. Much of my efforts are directed towards environmental art and level design, but more work is avaliable upon request! I have been working with Dark Tonic for about two years now- from an intern to a contracted designer, and have many different examples of work to showcase from it.

This is a continuation of the mines level as seen in editor. This level would use a new minecart enemy type that encompass the main threat as the player is positioned on the rocks. Because of the lack of flat land, this level is unique in that all of the threats are either moving constantly or flying.

Another level I created needed a fair bit more engineering to get going. In this level, you are in a car that's driving alongside a train which is moving slightly faster- and you must shoot all the husks and targets along the windows. I was responsible for figuring out how to give the illusion of movement that's in the background. I call what we did somewhat of a "flintstones" effect, where the train and player are stationary but all of the background elements cycle through several terrain slices. There are also prop spawners that place random shrubs and rocks closer to the camera to further randomize the backgroun while keeping it simple. This was a very fun project to work on!

This is the second part of the mines level, which is currently in the game. It is the only level that takes place in an interior, so it has a very different feeling from the rest of the levels. I was also responsible for setting up the new bat enemies which spawn and flow down toward the player, one of very few flying enemies in our game!
